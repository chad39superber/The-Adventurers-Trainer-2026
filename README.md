## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Adventurers**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and progression controls for the single-player campaign. It is designed around the game's party management, overworld travel, quests, resource management, and turn-based tactical combat systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the high-fantasy world, experiment with different party compositions, test classes and abilities, reduce resource management, or enjoy a more relaxed single-player campaign.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Travel across the overworld to reach towns, quest locations, ruins, forts, and other points of interest. Walking conserves supplies, while running consumes supplies and can increase the chance of reaching dangerous nighttime encounters. Use unlimited supplies when you want to travel freely without managing the normal movement costs.

### Tools & Inventory

Manage weapons, armor, magical items, consumables, and other equipment through the party inventory. Equipment condition matters during long journeys, so the durability option is useful when testing builds or preparing for extended quests. Unlimited gold can support recruitment, purchases, repairs, and other services.

### World & Environment

The campaign takes place in an evolving high-fantasy world with random events, towns, contracts, caravans, ruins, forests, mountains, and dangerous creatures. Accept quests through locations such as Town Hall, travel between objectives, and use the resource options to focus on exploration rather than supplies, morale, or upkeep.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based and tactical. Position the party carefully, use terrain to your advantage, and combine class abilities, spells, skills, and equipment. Health, energy, stamina, morale, and party composition all influence survival. Activate combat options before or during an encounter, then disable them when you want the standard difficulty and progression experience.
